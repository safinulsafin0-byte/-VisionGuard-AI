# VisionGuard AI — Responsible Use & Operational Governance

## 1. Ethical Principles & Human Oversight

VisionGuard AI is constructed under a **Human-In-The-Loop (HITL)** paradigm. It is strictly engineered to augment human domain expertise rather than replace human inspection personnel.

### Key Governance Policies
1. **Advisory Role**: VisionGuard AI does not make autonomous binding decisions regarding product release or scrap.
2. **Transparent Reasoning**: All warnings and alerts are paired with the mathematical indicator, measured value, and baseline threshold used to generate the notice.
3. **No Fabricated Certainty**: When visual indicators are ambiguous or borderline, the system explicitly routes the finding for human review rather than guessing.

## 2. Privacy & Data Protection

- **Local-First Architecture**: Media processing runs entirely within local host memory.
- **Zero Involuntary Telemetry**: VisionGuard AI contains no hidden telemetry beacons, usage tracking, or cloud sync services.
- **No Residual Storage**: Uploaded files and extracted frames are processed in volatile memory and are cleaned immediately upon session completion.
- **Data Ownership**: All uploaded images, video streams, and generated audit reports remain the exclusive intellectual property of the user.

## 3. Workplace & Operator Considerations

- **Workforce Collaboration**: The tool is designed to reduce eye strain and repetitive fatigue for visual inspectors by highlighting regions with measurable surface contrast discontinuities.
- **Non-Punitive Design**: The system records audit logs of inspected parts, not operator speed or productivity metrics.

## 4. Material Limitations & Prohibited Uses

- **Safety-Critical Prohibition**: Do NOT use VisionGuard AI as the sole gatekeeper for aerospace life-support, nuclear containment, high-speed rail braking assemblies, or surgical medical devices.
- **Environmental Variations**: Drastic lighting shifts or dirty lenses require recalibrating thresholds in the application sidebar.
